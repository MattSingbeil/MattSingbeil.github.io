## Details

**Date:** December 12, 2026

**Location:** Canmore, Alberta

- **Ceremony:** Canmore Ranch [104 Bow Valley Trl, Canmore, AB T1W 1N8](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XEY4hfkyyCLpaAeZA)
- **Reception:** Cornerstone Theater: [125 Kananaskis Way, Canmore, AB T1W 2X2](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XhUkMXzwgrowqoC19)

**Dress code**:

We want everyone to be comfortable, so please plan for proper winter clothing; think down jackets, hats, gloves, winter boots, etc over your formal attire. We wanted a mountain wedding in the winter so please dress to be warm during the ceremony, your comfort is our number one priority! We plan to have group photos taken after the ceremony before heading to the reception so come prepared to shed a layer for a quick photo! We will also have access to a warming tent at the [Canmore Ranch](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XEY4hfkyyCLpaAeZA), heated by a wood fire. To avoid any evening attire acquiring a smokey smell we would recommend an overcoat of sorts either formal or a down puffy!

The plan is that the ceremony will be held outdoors at the [Canmore Ranch](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XEY4hfkyyCLpaAeZA) to take advantage of the stunning mountain scenery we’re lucky to call home. However, if it’s too cold, we may be forced to move the ceremony indoors to the [Cornerstone Theatre](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XhUkMXzwgrowqoC19).

No matter where the ceremony takes place, warmth and comfort are our priority. Please dress for winter conditions in the Rockies.

**Please note: While the distance between the ranch and the theatre isn’t far (~1.7 km), it may be quite cold. We strongly recommend arranging transportation to and from the ranch rather than walking.**

## Details

**Date:** December 12, 2026

**Location:** Canmore, Alberta

- **Ceremony:** Canmore Ranch [104 Bow Valley Trl, Canmore, AB T1W 1N8](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XEY4hfkyyCLpaAeZA)
- **Reception:** Cornerstone Theater: [125 Kananaskis Way, Canmore, AB T1W 2X2](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XhUkMXzwgrowqoC19)

**Dress code**: Whatever makes you feel good and warm!
We want everyone to be comfortable, so please plan for proper winter clothing; think winter boots, down jackets, hats, gloves, etc.

The plan is that the ceremony will be held outdoors at the [Canmore Ranch](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XEY4hfkyyCLpaAeZA) to take advantage of the stunning mountain scenery we’re lucky to call home. However, if it’s too cold, we may be forced to move the ceremony indoors to the [Cornerstone Theatre](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XhUkMXzwgrowqoC19).

No matter where the ceremony takes place, warmth and comfort are our priority. Please dress for winter conditions in the Rockies. We’ll have access to a warming tent at the [Canmore Ranch](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XEY4hfkyyCLpaAeZA), heated by a wood fire. Because of this, there’s a chance clothing may smell smoky, so we don’t expect everyone to wear their reception attire to the ceremony.

**Please note: While the distance between the ranch and the theatre isn’t far (~1.7 km), it may be quite cold. We strongly recommend arranging transportation to and from the ranch rather than walking.**
